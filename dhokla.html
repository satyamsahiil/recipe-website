Khaman Dhokla (Gujarat)

Prep Time: 25 Mins Difficulty: Easy Type: Veg

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups Gram Flour (Besan)
  • Citric acid or Lemon juice, Sugar, Salt
  • Eno (Fruit Salt) for fluffiness
  • Tadka: Mustard seeds, Sesame seeds, Green chillies

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Mix besan, water, sugar, and lemon juice into a smooth batter.
  2. Add Eno just before steaming and pour into a greased tin.
  3. Steam for 15-20 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
  4. Pour a tempered sweet-spicy water (Tadka) over the sponge and garnish with coriander.
