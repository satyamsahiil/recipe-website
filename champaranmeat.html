Champaran Handi Meat

Prep Time: 1-4 hrs Difficulty: Difficult Type: Non Veg

Ingredients

  • Mutton: 1 kg (curry cut with bone).
  • Spices: Whole Spices: Bay leaves, black and green cardamom, cloves, cinnamon stick, dry red chillies.
  • Ground Spices: Turmeric (1 tsp), red chilli powder (2 tsp), coriander powder (2 tbsp), cumin powder (2 tsp), garam masala (1 tsp), and salt to taste.
  • Onions: 500g to 750g (thinly sliced or crushed). Garlic: 2–4 whole garlic bulbs (left whole) and 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste. Mustard Oil: ½ to 1 cup (authenticity requires pre-heating to smoking point, then cooling). Yogurt: ½ to 1 cup (optional, for tenderising). Ghee: 1–2 tbsp (for greasing the pot).

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Marinate: Mix 1kg mutton with 500g sliced onions, ginger-garlic paste, whole spices, powdered spices, and ½ cup smoked mustard oil; rest for 2 hours.
  2. Prep Pot: Grease a clay handi with ghee, add the marinated mixture, and drop in 3–4 whole garlic bulbs.
  3. Seal: Place the lid on the handi and seal the edges airtight using a long strip of wheat dough (atta).
  4. Slow Cook: Cook on a very low flame for 1.5 to 2 hours, shaking the pot gently every 20 minutes without opening the seal.
  5. Serve: Let it rest for 15 minutes, break the dough seal, and serve hot with rice or litti.
