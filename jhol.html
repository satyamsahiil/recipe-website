Bengali Machher Jhol (West Bengal)

Prep Time: 40 Mins Difficulty: Medium Type: Non-Veg

Ingredients

  • Fresh Fish (Rohu or Katla)
  • Mustard Oil (Mandatory for authentic taste)
  • Potatoes and Cauliflower (optional)
  • Panch Phoron (Five-spice mix) and Turmeric

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Marinate fish with turmeric and salt, then shallow fry in mustard oil.
  2. In the same oil, add panch phoron and sauté ginger-cumin paste.
  3. Add water to make a thin gravy and bring to a boil.
  4. Add the fried fish and simmer for 5 minutes. Garnish with green chillies.
