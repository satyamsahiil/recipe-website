Crispy Masala Dosa (Tamil Nadu)

Prep Time: 30 Mins* Difficulty: Medium Type: Veg

Ingredients

  • Fermented Rice and Urad Dal Batter
  • Boiled Potatoes (for stuffing)
  • Curry Leaves, Mustard Seeds, Turmeric
  • Sambar and Coconut Chutney (for serving)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prepare "Aloo Masala" by sautéing boiled potatoes with mustard seeds and curry leaves.
  2. Heat a non-stick tawa and spread a thin ladle of batter in a circular motion.
  3. Drizzle oil/ghee around the edges until golden and crispy.
  4. Place the potato filling in the center, fold, and serve hot.
