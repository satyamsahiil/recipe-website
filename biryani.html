Hyderabadi Dum Biryani

Time: 90 Mins | Difficulty: Hard

Ingredients

  • 500g Basmati Rice
  • 1kg Chicken/Mutton
  • Fried Onions (Birista)
  • Saffron and Mint Leaves

Instructions

  1. Marinate the meat with yogurt and spices for 4 hours.
  2. Parboil rice with whole spices until 70% cooked.
  3. Layer the meat and rice in a heavy-bottomed pot.
  4. Seal with dough (Dum) and cook on low heat for 45 mins.
